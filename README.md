## Introduction
Hello there. I'm a Microsoft MVP, architect, author, and speaker with over 25 years of experience in Microsoft messaging, identity, and collaboration technologies. I specialize in Microsoft Exchange, Exchange Online, and email security, with a strong focus on architecture, migration, automation, and practical solutions. I authored several Microsoft Exchange books, including [Pro Exchange Server Administration](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4842-9591-5), and contribute to the [Microsoft 365 for IT Pros](https://o365itpros.gumroad.com/l/O365IT) book series. I regularly share knowledge, tools, and real-world experience with the Microsoft community or through my blog, [eightwone.com](https://eightwone.com).
